La Résistance : des refus isolés aux réseaux (1940–1944)

D · Grammar: passé composé, imparfait or plus-que-parfait?

Spot the pattern first. Tell the story of the Resistance in the past:

  • Les premiers résistants agissaient souvent seuls. — background, what usually happened
  • En mai 1943, Jean Moulin a réuni les mouvements. — one completed event
  • Quand il a été arrêté, il avait déjà réussi sa mission. — an event before another past event

Choose the right tense in each sentence.

E · Translation into French

Translate into French.

At first, the resisters acted alone and many of them were arrested. In 1943, thousands of young men who wanted to avoid the STO joined the maquis. Although the Resistance was a minority, it helped to liberate France.
