D · Grammar: the passé simple

Spot the pattern first. Three verbs from the texts:

  • En 1791, les esclaves se révoltèrent. (se révolter)
  • En 1804, la colonie devint Haïti. (devenir)
  • Des musiciens eurent l'idée de mêler ces rythmes. (avoir)

The passé simple is the written, literary past tense for completed events — in speech you would say se sont révoltés, est devenue, ont eu. You only need to recognise it at A-Level, but using it in a history essay is a mark of style. Notice the effect too: the texts switch to it for the great dated events. Choose the passé simple form in each sentence.

E · Translation into French

Translate into French.

Haitian music reflects the history of the country. While the elite listened to European dances, most Haitians kept African traditions alive. Today, kompa links the diaspora to Haiti, and many young people hope that it will never disappear.

Help

refléter · tandis que / alors que · l'élite écoutait · garder vivantes les traditions · relier la diaspora à Haïti · espérer que + indicatif (futur)
