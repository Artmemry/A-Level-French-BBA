Marking principles applied

MARKING PRINCIPLES — translation into French
Accents: grammatical accent errors count as mistakes (e.g. je suis alle). Non-grammatical accent errors are tolerated (e.g. j'espere for j'espère) unless they cause ambiguity — ou rather than où, and a rather than à, are not tolerated.
Spelling: minor spelling errors are tolerated as long as they are not ambiguous and do not form a word in the wrong language.
Verb endings, genders and adjective endings must be correct and are not treated as spelling errors.
Repeated capitalisation errors are penalised once only. Other repeated or consequential errors are not penalised.
Any appropriate alternative that does not appear in the Accept column is credited.
How this page marks. It checks that every element of an acceptable answer is present — in any order, allowing synonyms, tolerated accent errors and minor stem misspellings — and withholds the mark when the Reject column applies or a required element is missing. The real mark scheme also credits any appropriate alternative a human examiner judges correct, and no program can reproduce that. So where you disagree, read the mark scheme for that chunk and award yourself the mark with a justification. Your download shows both totals and your reasoning, and your teacher decides.
