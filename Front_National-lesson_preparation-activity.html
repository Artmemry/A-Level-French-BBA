Du Front national au Rassemblement national (1972–2026)

How did a party that won 0.75% in 1974 become the largest single party in the Assemblée nationale fifty years later? D · Grammar: comparatives and superlatives of adverbs

Spot the pattern first:

  • Le RN progresse plus rapidement que les autres partis.
  • Le FN de 1974 réussissait moins bien que le RN d'aujourd'hui.
  • C'est chez les jeunes que le RN a progressé le plus vite.

Comparative: plus / moins / aussi + adverb + que. Superlative: le plus / le moins + adverb — with adverbs it is always le, never la or les. Irregular: bien → mieux → le mieux; mal → plus mal; beaucoup → plus → le plus. Choose the correct form.

E · Translation into French

Translate into French.

The National Front was founded in 1972, but it was only in the 1980s that it made its breakthrough. In 2018 it changed its name. Although the party is stronger than ever, many voters still refuse to vote for it, and the 2027 election will show whether this barrier still exists.
