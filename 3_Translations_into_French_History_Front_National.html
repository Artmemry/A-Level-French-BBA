Exercise 1: A party on the margins (1972–1999)

English Text (131 words):

The National Front was founded in October 1972 on the initiative of a far-right movement. Jean-Marie Le Pen took over its leadership. At first, the party had very little success: in 1974, Le Pen won only 0.75% of the vote. The breakthrough came in the mid-1980s. In 1984, the FN won more than 10% at the European elections and, two years later, 35 FN deputies entered the Assembly thanks to proportional representation. The party made immigration its central theme, linking it to crime and unemployment. However, its leader's provocative remarks kept it isolated from the other parties. In 1998, his deputy Bruno Mégret caused a split and left with many of the party's members. Many observers thought that the FN would not survive. They were wrong. The party would soon recover.

Exercise 2: From shock to « dédiabolisation » (2002–2018)

English Text (146 words):

On 21 April 2002, Jean-Marie Le Pen caused a shock by qualifying for the second round of the presidential election. Hundreds of thousands of people demonstrated against him and Jacques Chirac was re-elected with more than 82% of the vote. In 2011, his daughter Marine succeeded him and set out to make the party more respectable. This strategy is called « dédiabolisation ». She talked more about purchasing power and social protection, while keeping « national priority » at the heart of the programme. In 2015, she even expelled her own father after new remarks about the Second World War. The FN came first in the 2014 European elections and in 2018 it changed its name to become the Rassemblement national. Its opponents believe that nothing has changed in substance; its supporters reply that the party has simply become normal. The debate is far from over.

Exercise 3: At the gates of power? (2022–2026)

English Text (138 words):

In 2022, Marine Le Pen won 41% of the vote in the second round against Emmanuel Macron, and 89 RN deputies were elected. Two years later, the list led by Jordan Bardella won the European elections with over 31% of the vote. That same evening, the President dissolved the National Assembly. The RN came first in the first round, but other parties joined forces against it and it finished third. Even so, it now has more than 120 deputies. In March 2025, Marine Le Pen was convicted of embezzling European public funds and barred from standing for election with immediate effect. In July 2026, the appeal court confirmed her guilt but shortened her ban. She may therefore stand in the 2027 presidential election. No one knows yet whether the « republican front » will hold one more time.
